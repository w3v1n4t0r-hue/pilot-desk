Accelerated Stalls & Load Factor

In a coordinated level turn, the wings must produce more lift than in straight-and-level flight. That increased load factor raises stall speed.

Load factor

Load factor n = 1 ÷ cos(bank angle)

At 60° of bank in a coordinated level turn, the load factor is 2 G.

Accelerated stall speed

New stall speed = 1-G stall speed × √n

If the 1-G stall speed is 50 kt, a 2-G load factor increases the theoretical stall speed to about 71 kt. Actual aircraft limitations, configuration and published stall information still control.

Why it matters

A steep turn can place the airplane much closer to an accelerated stall than indicated airspeed alone might suggest. The relationship is especially important when maneuvering close to the ground, where recovery margin is limited.
