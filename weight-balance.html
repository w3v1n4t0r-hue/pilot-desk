What the calculator is doing

For each station, moment is weight multiplied by arm. Total center of gravity is total moment divided by total weight. PilotDesk performs that calculation independently for the departure and landing columns so a fuel burn or other in-flight weight change can be represented without rebuilding the entire table.

Show formulas
Station moment = station weight × station arm
Total weight = Σ station weights
Total moment = Σ station moments
CG = total moment ÷ total weight

Before using the result

Verify basic empty weight and moment/arm against the current aircraft weight-and-balance record, use the correct station arms, account for required occupants, baggage, fuel and equipment, and compare both departure and landing conditions with every applicable approved limit. If actual weights are available, they are preferable to generic standard weights.
