What this page does

Use one airport lookup instead of entering the same identifier again on the weather, procedures, and planner pages. You can also save the airport for quicker access later.

Airport and station search uses U.S. Aviation Weather Center station data. Weather is requested through PilotDesk's live weather endpoint, with NOAA/NWS text products available as a weather fallback. FAA procedure links come from the FAA d-TPP source used by PilotDesk's procedure viewer.
