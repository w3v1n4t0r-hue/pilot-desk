How to use the Fuel Weight calculator

Enter the fuel volume and the density appropriate to the fuel and temperature assumption. The conversion is only as good as that density.

Inputs

  • Fuel volume — enter the value in US gal.
  • Fuel density — enter the value in lb/gal.

Formula and method

Fuel weight = volume × entered fuel density.

What the result means

Fuel weight helps with loading calculations; it does not determine usable capacity or the correct fuel quantity for a specific airplane.

Common mistakes to avoid

Do not assume every fuel type weighs the same per gallon. Use the aircraft documentation and the density or planning value appropriate to the situation.
