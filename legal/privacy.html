Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026. PilotDesk is designed to work with as little personal information as practical. This policy explains what may be stored or processed when you use the site.

Information stored on your device

Aircraft profiles, favorites, recent tools, recent calculation summaries and similar convenience settings are stored in your browser's local storage. PilotDesk does not intentionally transmit those aircraft-profile values or calculator inputs to a PilotDesk account or database. Clearing browser/site data may delete them.

Weather requests

When you request airport weather, the station identifier is sent to a PilotDesk server endpoint, which requests METAR, TAF and airport information from the U.S. Aviation Weather Center. The request may be visible in normal hosting logs. Do not enter sensitive personal information into the station field.

Analytics

PilotDesk uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate traffic such as page views, referring sites, device/browser information and general geographic information provided by that service. PilotDesk does not use analytics to read aircraft profiles or calculator input fields.

Advertising and consent

PilotDesk uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure and personalize advertising where permitted. Where required, a consent message may give you choices about advertising and related data processing. Google's own privacy policies and consent controls apply to its services.

Error reporting

To find broken pages and JavaScript failures, PilotDesk may send a limited error report to its hosting logs. That report is designed to contain the error message, technical stack information and the page path. It is not designed to include aircraft profiles, calculator inputs or form contents.

Server logs

Hosting, security and advertising providers may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, requested URL and security-related information as part of operating their services.

External links

PilotDesk links to third-party sources, including government aviation resources. Once you leave PilotDesk, the destination site's policies apply.

Children

PilotDesk is a general aviation utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly create accounts or intentionally collect personal information from children through PilotDesk.

Changes

This policy may be updated as PilotDesk changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Questions

Privacy questions can be submitted through the PilotDesk feedback page.

This policy describes PilotDesk's current practices and is not a promise that third-party providers will never change their own processing.
