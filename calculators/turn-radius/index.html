How to use the Turn Radius calculator

Enter true airspeed and bank angle to estimate the radius and diameter of a coordinated level turn.

Inputs

  • True airspeed — enter the value in kt.
  • Bank angle — enter the value in deg.

Formula and method

Coordinated turn radius is determined by true airspeed and bank angle.

What the result means

The radius is the horizontal distance around the idealized turn. The calculated rate is included to show the same geometry from another angle.

Common mistakes to avoid

The estimate excludes wind and assumes a coordinated level turn. Ground track radius will differ when wind is present.
