About links to PilotDesk

No link scheme

You do not have to give PilotDesk a ranking-credit backlink to use a widget. The small source link inside the widget is marked nofollow. If you independently find PilotDesk useful enough to recommend, you may link to the specific calculator, guide, E6B, or training page that is most relevant to your students. There is no reciprocal-link requirement and no preferred keyword-rich anchor text.

That keeps the relationship simple: use the tool because it helps your students, and link to PilotDesk only when the link itself is useful to your visitors.

Important operating note

Supplemental training aid

PilotDesk is designed for planning, instruction, and study. It is not a substitute for current approved aircraft data, official weather, procedures, regulations, NOTAMs, operator guidance, or instructor judgment. For operational decisions, verify the numbers and source material that apply to the specific aircraft and flight.
