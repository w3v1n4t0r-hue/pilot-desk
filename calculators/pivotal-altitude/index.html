How to use the Pivotal Altitude calculator

Pivotal Altitude: Use the result to study how these values work together. Follow the aircraft limitations and the procedure taught for the maneuver.

Inputs

  • Groundspeed — enter the value in kt.

Formula and method

Pivotal altitude (ft AGL) ≈ groundspeed² ÷ 11.3 for the common knots-and-feet training rule.

What the result means

Pivotal altitude: The result shows the modeled relationship. A real maneuver also depends on how the airplane is flown.

Common mistakes to avoid

Pivotal Altitude check: Confirm whether the formula expects true airspeed, indicated airspeed, groundspeed or a particular bank-angle assumption.
