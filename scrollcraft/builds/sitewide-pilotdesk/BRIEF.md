# PilotDesk sitewide Scrollcraft brief

Based on the supplied requirements and the approved homepage. Implementation decisions below are authored, not attributed quotations.

1. Vibe: black-first, precise, dimensional, aviation-specific. Existing PilotDesk logo, typography, palette and the approved physical-paper/E6B/radar/aircraft scenes are the reference assets.
2. User scope: “crawl then entire website and add animated buttons and premium scroll driven pages for everything”. The crawl found 157 public sitemap routes, including 47 calculators and 73 guides.
3. User direction: “Make every page equally cinematic”. Authored journey: recognize the task, see its geometry resolve, enter the real workspace or article, understand the method, continue to a useful next destination.
4. Energy: dimensional opening, decisive handoff, calm working surface, editorial rhythm through reference content, clear close. No extra empty pinned screens.
5. Signature: a flight briefing that resolves under the reader's hand. Route strokes, instrument scales and independent visual planes resolve as the opening leaves the viewport.
6. Range: premium minimal, retaining the canonical PilotDesk system. Equal ambition on every page, different scenes by subject. No fake live data or new numeric claims.
7. Structure: distinct page-family scenes. Existing navigation remains real; no scroll interception. A direct workspace/read link bypasses the opening.
8. Assets: supplied and approved existing chart, E6B, radar, book and Cessna artwork; new native SVG diagrams. No generated media, stock purchases or new external services.

## Journey and feeling curve

Recognition: the original page heading and useful description establish the task.
Curiosity: independent diagram layers separate as scrolling begins.
Clarity (peak): the task-specific instrument or route geometry resolves, then recedes to reveal the working page.
Confidence: actual inputs, results, diagrams and explanations remain stable and readable.
Resolve: original related links and actions carry the visitor onward.

Peak sentence: “The page turns the flight problem into a physical picture before I use the tool.”
Tell-someone sentence: “It's the site where the briefing becomes the workspace as you scroll.”
Authored silence: forms, charts, tables, map controls and live results do not drift while being used.

## Grammar

Flight briefing (authored grammar): original shared nav; a dimensional subject-specific title scene; direct handoff to the real tool/article; chapter reveals and an unobtrusive reading rail; original functional ending. Bans: scroll hijacking, artificial waiting, fake telemetry, transformed map ancestors, hidden essential content, repeated long pinning.

The seven kit alternatives do not fit the entire product: filmic one-shot and continuous world add a long passive sequence; pure editorial is insufficient for tools; live surface forbids the requested cinematic opening; gallery, catalog and spatial exhibition foreground browsing over a focused pilot task. The authored grammar retains the working surface while giving every route cinematic treatment.

Far plane: restrained contour/grid field, small movement.
Middle plane: scene-specific geometry, intermediate travel.
Subject: E6B, radar, study material, fuel/altitude/loading diagram, deliberate rotation and scale.
Foreground: route/diagram trace, greater relative displacement without crossing copy.
Typography and actions: stable semantic HTML, separate from artwork.
