Aviation Calculator Widgets for Flight School Websites

A flight school website can be more useful to students when it contains interactive training resources instead of only schedules and contact information. PilotDesk provides embeddable aviation tools designed for flight-school, CFI and flying-club websites.

What is an aviation calculator widget?

A widget is a small interactive tool that can appear inside another website. Instead of sending students through several menus, a school can place a relevant PilotDesk calculator near its training resources.

Who can use the widgets?

The embed tools are intended for flight schools, independent CFIs, university aviation organizations, flying clubs and other aviation education sites that want convenient student resources.

Why create an embedded tool library?

Dedicated tools give students a repeatable place to practice common aviation calculations. They can also complement lesson pages by linking directly to deeper PilotDesk explanations and related calculators.

Where to start

Visit the PilotDesk embed page for available widgets and implementation details. Schools can also link to the broader PilotDesk for Flight Schools page or the Flight Training hub.
