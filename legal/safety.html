SAFETY

How PilotDesk handles safety

PilotDesk is built to be useful without pretending a web calculator can replace approved aviation data or pilot judgment.

Fail closed when possible

Calculator inputs are validated and impossible or nonsensical relationships are rejected where practical. When a value cannot be calculated reliably from the entered data, PilotDesk should show an error or no result rather than silently invent a number.

Assumptions are shown

Many aviation formulas are approximations. PilotDesk identifies methods and assumptions so users can understand what a result means and where it stops being appropriate.

Aircraft-specific limits come from approved data

PilotDesk does not create V-speeds, structural limitations, takeoff/landing performance or approved weight-and-balance envelopes for a specific aircraft. Those values must come from the current POH/AFM, supplements, placards, approved operator data or other authoritative source applicable to that aircraft.

Weather is supplemental

Weather shown on PilotDesk is a convenience display, not a complete weather briefing. Pilots remain responsible for obtaining all information required for the operation, including NOTAMs and relevant forecasts/advisories.

Report problems

If a result, formula, page or weather display looks wrong, stop relying on it and report the problem. PilotDesk may log limited technical errors to help diagnose broken features.

Before flight

Recheck critical calculations independently. Verify current weather and airport information. Compare aircraft-specific numbers with approved data. If a result conflicts with a POH/AFM, regulation, official briefing or qualified instructor/operator guidance, the authoritative source controls.
