How to use the Climb Gradient to FPM calculator

Climb Gradient to FPM: Use the result as a math check or training reference. Aircraft performance, limitations and operating decisions still come from the current POH or AFM, applicable charts and the conditions that actually exist.

Inputs

  • Gradient — enter the value in ft/NM.
  • Groundspeed — enter the value in kt.

Formula and method

Vertical speed (ft/min) = climb gradient (ft/NM) × groundspeed (NM/min).

What the result means

Vertical speed: Performance math describes relationships, not guarantees. Weight, configuration, runway condition, wind, temperature, technique and aircraft-specific data can all change the operational answer.

Common mistakes to avoid

Climb Gradient to FPM check: Common errors include using the wrong weight or configuration, confusing indicated and true airspeed, applying a training shortcut outside its assumptions, or treating a calculated estimate as an aircraft limitation.
