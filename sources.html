Sources & Methods

PilotDesk uses standard aviation math, physics relationships and FAA-style training references. This page points to the primary references behind the site's methods. Aircraft-specific limitations and performance always come from the current approved data for that aircraft.

Formula notes

Some tools use exact geometric or physical relationships; others intentionally use common training approximations. Examples include coordinated-turn relationships, wind-vector trigonometry, hydroplaning speed ≈ 9√PSI, pivotal altitude ≈ groundspeed²/11.3 in feet for knots, and the square-root weight relationship for adjusted maneuvering speed. Each calculator should identify its method and assumptions on the calculator page.

Live weather limitations

PilotDesk requests weather server-side because the Aviation Weather Center does not permit browser cross-origin access to its Data API. Requests are kept narrow and may be cached briefly to reduce load. AWC publishes its own rate limits and availability rules. PilotDesk does not modify the raw METAR or TAF text before display.

Corrections

If you find a formula, explanation or source that appears wrong or outdated, please report it. Safety-related corrections should be treated as higher priority than feature requests.

Links above are provided for reference. Third-party and government sites control their own content and availability.
