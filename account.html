PRO · CLOUD BACKUP

Move aircraft and saved flights between devices.

Pro required

Pro accounts can back up the aircraft and saved flights stored in this browser to the PilotDesk account, then restore that snapshot on another device. Restore replaces the local aircraft and saved-flight lists on the device you are using, so PilotDesk asks before doing it.

PRO · TRAIN + FLY

Full checkride prep and a larger planning workspace.

Compare plans →

Pro includes all interactive oral/checkride prep subjects, examiner follow-up conversations, mock-orals, PDF checkride packets, removes the Free-plan cap on local aircraft profiles and saved flights, adds manual cloud backup/restore, and removes ads while signed in. Core calculators, current weather, public guides, Written Prep, Daily, and the checkride preview remain free.
