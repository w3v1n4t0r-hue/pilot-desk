Billing questions

Can I use PilotDesk without paying?

Yes. Core calculators, weather, route planning, study tools, and the free account remain available without Pro.

What does Pro add right now?

Manual cloud backup and restore for aircraft and saved flights, plus an ad-free signed-in experience.

Does Pro make PilotDesk an approved source?

No. Paid access does not change the limitations of PilotDesk. Verify operational information with current approved and official sources.

Can I cancel or update my card?

Yes. Active paid accounts can open Stripe billing management from the Account page.
