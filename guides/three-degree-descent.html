3° Descent Rate

A three-degree flight path is common in approach and descent planning. The required vertical speed depends on groundspeed: the faster the airplane moves over the ground, the faster it must descend to stay on the same angle.

Quick rule

Vertical speed ≈ groundspeed × 5

At 120 kt groundspeed, the quick estimate is about 600 fpm. PilotDesk also calculates the geometric value using a 3° angle, so you can compare the shortcut with the more exact result.

Distance rule

Another useful planning estimate is roughly 3 NM of descent for each 1,000 ft of altitude to lose. That is a planning shortcut, not a substitute for a published procedure or stabilized-approach criteria.
