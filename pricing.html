WHAT STAYS FREE

Basic aviation information should not become a subscription trap.

Core calculators, source links, safety information, and basic study material remain available without Pro. The paid product is the convenience of continuity: sync, history, organization, and instructor workflows.

WHAT EARNS THE FEE

Pro should save time every week.

If Pro does not make active training or planning meaningfully easier, it should not be paid. The launch target is intentionally simple and inexpensive while the higher-value features are proven.

Before billing turns on

Can I use PilotDesk without paying?

Yes. The core site remains free.

Will existing free features suddenly disappear?

The plan is to monetize sync, deeper history, organization, and school workflows—not remove the basic tools that brought pilots to PilotDesk.

Why is checkout not live yet?

PilotDesk is building the entitlement and account experience first. Charging before the paid experience is clearly better would be backwards.
