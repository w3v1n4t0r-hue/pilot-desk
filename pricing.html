Billing questions

Can I use PilotDesk without paying?

Yes. Core calculators, current weather lookup, public guides, Daily, Written Prep, Weak Subjects, route-planning basics, one aircraft profile, three saved flights, and a two-topic checkride preview for every rating remain free.

What exactly is paywalled?

The full interactive checkride/oral-prep set and Checkride Lab are Pro. Free users can try two oral subjects in every rating. Pro adds examiner follow-up conversations, mock-orals, debriefs, and PDF checkride packets. Public checkride study pages and FAA-source reference articles remain open.

What happens if I already have more than the Free aircraft or flight limit?

PilotDesk does not delete existing local data. You can keep using, editing, exporting, or deleting what is already saved; Pro is required to create additional aircraft profiles or saved flights above the Free limit.

Does Pro include Written Prep?

Written Prep core modes remain free. Pro includes the full Checkride Lab, source-based Study Plans, the Pro Preflight workspace, expanded saved planning workflows, cloud backup/restore, and an ad-free signed-in experience.

How is paid access controlled?

Stripe manages payment and subscription changes. PilotDesk grants paid access only after the server receives a verified subscription event, so changing browser storage or profile fields does not grant Pro access.

Can I cancel or update my card?

Yes. Active paid accounts can open Stripe billing management from the PilotDesk account page.
