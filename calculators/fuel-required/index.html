How to use the Fuel Required calculator

Fuel Required: Try the values from a route or training scenario. Check that wind direction, course reference, speed, time and fuel units match your source.

Inputs

  • Fuel on board — enter the value in gal.
  • Fuel burn — enter the value in gal/hr.
  • Trip time — enter the value in hr.
  • Reserve — enter the value in min.

Formula and method

Trip fuel = fuel flow × trip time. Total required fuel adds the entered reserve quantity before comparing the result with fuel on board.

What the result means

Required fuel: Groundspeed affects estimated time and fuel. Wind direction also changes the headwind, tailwind or crosswind component.

Common mistakes to avoid

Fuel Required check: Check whether a calculation calls for airspeed or groundspeed, and convert minutes to hours before multiplying by knots.
