HOW TO USE IT

Work the source, not a memorized answer.

1Choose your rating

PPL, Instrument, CPL, CFI and ATP use the applicable FAA ACS. CFII remains mapped to FAA-S-8081-9E.

2Use the exact FAA figure when provided

Figure questions point to the official FAA testing supplement rather than a redrawn or invented chart.

3Read every rationale

After grading, PilotDesk explains why each choice works or fails and shows the controlling reference.

4Review weak elements

Scores are grouped by FAA standard so repeated misses lead directly to the area that needs more study.

FAA-source written test prep built for accuracy

PilotDesk Written Prep deliberately uses a smaller reviewed bank instead of thousands of formula-spun variants. The live questions are exact public FAA samples or manually authored PilotDesk questions calibrated to FAA sample-question structure and tied to current ACS/PTS elements.

Where the FAA uses a testing-supplement figure, PilotDesk uses that same FAA figure reference. Figure questions identify the supplement and figure number, and the answer review explains the reasoning behind all three choices.

PilotDesk does not claim to reproduce the FAA’s confidential active question bank or a commercial test-prep company’s proprietary database. Current FAA regulations, standards, handbooks, charts, testing supplements, and aircraft-approved data remain the controlling sources.
