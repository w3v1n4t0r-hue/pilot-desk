Climb Gradient to FPM

Departure and obstacle requirements are often expressed in feet per nautical mile, while the cockpit vertical-speed indicator shows feet per minute. Groundspeed connects the two.

Formula

Required FPM = climb gradient (ft/NM) × groundspeed (kt) ÷ 60

At 90 kt groundspeed, a 200 ft/NM requirement works out to 300 fpm. If groundspeed increases, the required vertical speed also increases because the airplane covers each nautical mile faster.

What pilots can miss

The conversion uses groundspeed, not indicated airspeed. Wind therefore changes the vertical speed needed to maintain a given climb gradient over the ground. The calculation does not prove the airplane can achieve that performance.

Before using it operationally

Compare required performance with current approved aircraft performance data, conditions, weight, procedures and any applicable obstacle-departure or operator requirements.
