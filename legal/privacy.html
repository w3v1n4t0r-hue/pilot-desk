PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026. PilotDesk collects as little personal information as practical for the features it provides. Most personal app data stays in your browser rather than being stored in a PilotDesk account.

Information stored on your device

Aircraft profiles, favorites, recently used tools, recent calculation summaries, usage preferences and similar convenience settings are stored in your browser's local storage. PilotDesk does not send aircraft-profile values or calculator inputs to a PilotDesk account or database during normal use. Clearing browser/site data may remove them.

If you use the aircraft export feature, your browser creates a JSON backup file on your device. PilotDesk does not receive that file unless you independently choose to send it somewhere.

Weather requests

When you request airport weather, the station identifier is sent to a PilotDesk server endpoint, which requests METAR, TAF and airport information from the U.S. Aviation Weather Center. The station identifier and normal request metadata may appear in hosting logs. Do not enter personal information in the station field.

Analytics and performance

PilotDesk uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to understand site traffic and real-world performance. These services may process information such as page views, referrers, browser/device type, general location derived by the service, and page-performance measurements. PilotDesk also records limited product events such as a calculator being used, a weather search, an aircraft profile being saved, an install prompt being used, or a guide leading to a calculator. These events identify the feature or page, not the numerical values you entered.

PilotDesk is configured not to send aircraft names, tail numbers, passenger information, calculator inputs, Weight & Balance entries, or raw profile data as analytics-event properties.

Advertising and consent

PilotDesk uses Google AdSense to help support the free app. Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to serve, measure and, where permitted, personalize advertising. Where required, a consent message may provide choices about advertising and related data processing. Google's policies and consent controls apply to Google's services.

Error reporting

To find broken pages and JavaScript failures, PilotDesk may send a limited error report to hosting logs. The report is intended to include the error message, technical stack information and page path. It excludes aircraft profiles, calculator inputs and form contents from the report format.

Server and security logs

Hosting, security and advertising providers may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, requested URL and security-related information as part of operating their services.

Accounts and payments

PilotDesk currently does not require a PilotDesk account and does not directly collect payment-card information. If account or paid features are added later, this policy will be updated before those features are relied on for personal data or billing.

External links

PilotDesk links to third-party sources, including FAA and other government aviation resources. Once you leave PilotDesk, the destination site's policies apply.

Children

PilotDesk is a general aviation utility and is not directed to children under 13. PilotDesk does not knowingly create child accounts or knowingly collect personal information from children through an account system.

Changes

This policy may be updated as PilotDesk changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Questions

Privacy questions can be submitted through the PilotDesk feedback page.

This page describes PilotDesk's current practices. Third-party providers control their own systems and policies.
