How Much Does Avgas Weigh per Gallon?

For quick planning, pilots often use about six pounds per gallon. The more precise FAA standard value commonly referenced at 59°F is about 6.01 lb/US gal, and the reason for the “about” matters.

The familiar number is a standard planning weight

The FAA Weight & Balance Handbook lists standard aviation-fuel weights that vary with temperature. A commonly used standard value for aviation gasoline is about 6.01 lb per U.S. gallon at 59°F. That is useful for training and rough planning, but it is not a promise that every gallon in every tank weighs exactly 6.01 pounds.

Quick examples

FuelAt 6.01 lb/US gal
10 gal60.1 lb
20 gal120.2 lb
30 gal180.3 lb
40 gal240.4 lb
50 gal300.5 lb

For a real loading calculation, use the value specified by the aircraft/manufacturer/operator when one is provided, and remember that fuel density changes with temperature and fuel grade.
