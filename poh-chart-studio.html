What the current engine models

This release handles a common two-dimensional performance-chart pattern: an X axis, a Y axis, and a family of parameter curves. PilotDesk converts calibrated pixels into chart coordinates, interpolates along the traced curves, then interpolates between adjacent family values. If the requested family value or X location is outside the traced data, the calculation is rejected rather than extrapolated.

Some POH/AFM charts are chained nomographs with several hand-off regions, nonlinear scales, or multiple correction sections. Those require a separately verified multi-stage model. This page does not silently pretend a simple 2-D trace is equivalent to those charts. Keep the original chart available and compare several published/manual examples before treating an imported model as useful.

Copyright and privacy

The source chart image is read locally in your browser and is not included when PilotDesk saves or exports the mathematical trace model. Only use charts you are entitled to possess. A saved model is a convenience copy of your own calibration and does not grant redistribution rights to the underlying POH/AFM page.
