Weather data behavior

PilotDesk requests live weather server-side from the Aviation Weather Center and NOAA/NWS. AWC is preferred; NOAA/NWS can supply METAR and TAF text automatically when AWC is slow or unavailable. Nearby stations use the AWC station index and are shown by straight-line distance only.
