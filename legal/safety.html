TRUST & SAFETY

Useful aviation tools, with clear limits.

PilotDesk is a planning and training aid. It is not an FAA-approved navigation source, official weather briefing, aircraft flight manual, dispatch release, ATC clearance, checklist, or emergency service.

PilotDesk is built to make aviation math easier to inspect without making a web app look more authoritative than it is. The goal is simple: show the method, reject bad inputs where practical, identify the source of live data, and make uncertainty visible.

What PilotDesk can be trusted to do

PilotDesk can perform the stated calculator math from the values entered, display the formula or method used, show live-data source information when available, and preserve local convenience data such as favorites and aircraft profiles in your browser. Automated regression tests cover calculator formulas, JavaScript syntax, local links, site integrity, and core product behavior before changes are merged.

What PilotDesk must not replace

Do not use PilotDesk as the sole source for a go/no-go decision, aircraft limitation, performance number, weight-and-balance approval, runway suitability decision, legal weather briefing, navigation decision, maintenance determination, or emergency action. If PilotDesk conflicts with a current POH/AFM, supplement, placard, regulation, NOTAM, official weather product, chart, operator procedure, or qualified instructor/operator guidance, use the authoritative source.

Calculator safety

Calculator inputs are range-checked and impossible or nonsensical relationships are rejected where practical. If a calculation cannot be produced reliably from the entered values, PilotDesk should show an error or no result instead of silently inventing a number. A result can still be mathematically correct and operationally inappropriate, so aircraft-specific limits and performance always need to be checked separately.

Formula and assumption transparency

Many aviation formulas are approximations or training shortcuts. Calculator pages identify the formula, units, assumptions, and known limits so the result is not presented as a black box. PilotDesk does not create unpublished V-speeds, structural limits, approved performance data, or an approved weight-and-balance envelope for an aircraft.

Weather freshness and source status

PilotDesk weather is supplemental. The weather page displays source and retrieval information and shows the reported observation time when available. A METAR that appears more than 90 minutes old is flagged so an older observation does not look equivalent to a fresh one. Source outages, partial responses, backup-source use, and offline status are also surfaced when detected.

Even a fresh METAR or TAF is not a complete briefing. Verify current weather, NOTAMs, runway information, forecasts, advisories, and all other information required for the operation using the appropriate official or approved sources.

Ads do not control the app

Advertising is isolated from PilotDesk's calculator, saved-data, theme, sharing, error-handling, and product code. If an ad is blocked or fails to load, core PilotDesk features are intended to continue working. Advertising content does not change calculator formulas or weather results.

Privacy by minimization

PilotDesk does not require an account. Aircraft profiles, favorites, recent tools, calculation summaries, and similar convenience data are stored locally in the browser during normal use. Analytics events are limited to feature-level activity and are configured not to include calculator values, Weight & Balance entries, aircraft names, tail numbers, passenger information, or raw aircraft-profile data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

No emergency use

PilotDesk is not monitored as an emergency service and should not be used to request emergency assistance or time-critical operational support. In an aviation emergency, use the applicable aircraft procedures and established emergency, ATC, company, airport, or public-safety channels.

Report a safety problem

If a result, formula, source, airport record, weather display, or page looks wrong, stop relying on that output and report it. Choose Safety-critical issue for anything that could plausibly mislead a pilot. Do not include passwords, private passenger information, or sensitive security details in a public GitHub report.

Before using a result for flight planning

Check the inputs and units. Confirm the source and age of live data. Rework critical arithmetic independently. Compare aircraft-specific values with current approved data. Check whether the calculation's assumptions fit the real situation. When there is uncertainty, treat PilotDesk as the secondary cross-check—not the controlling source.
