HOW THE STUDY LOOP WORKS

Built to make misses disappear — and show exactly where they live.

1Choose your certificate

PPL, Instrument, CPL, CFI and ATP use their current FAA ACS. CFII uses the current FAA instrument-instructor PTS.

2Answer three real distractors

Every item has three choices. Wrong answers are built around plausible misconceptions, nearby limits, or calculation errors.

3See the standard

Each item displays its ACS or PTS reference and difficulty, then feeds that element’s running performance.

4Get graded

Sessions receive a letter grade and pass/fail against the track’s 70% knowledge-test threshold. Readiness separately factors in bank coverage.

Free FAA written test prep with 5,000 ACS-mapped practice items

PilotDesk Written Prep contains 1,000 practice items each for Private Pilot Airplane (PAR), Instrument Rating Airplane (IRA), Commercial Pilot Airplane (CAX), Flight Instructor Airplane (FIA), and Airline Transport Pilot Multiengine Airplane (ATM). Those 5,000 items are tagged to the applicable current FAA ACS document and knowledge element. An additional 250 CFII practice items are mapped to FAA-S-8081-9E because the FAA currently maintains Flight Instructor Instrument under the PTS rather than an ACS.

Every practice item uses exactly three answer choices. The bank combines reviewed scenario and calculation families with parameterized variations so pilots see the same underlying knowledge from different angles instead of memorizing a single number or button position. Foundation, applied and advanced filters let a pilot deliberately increase difficulty. Adaptive study, missed-question review, standards-level grading and practice exams are saved to a free PilotDesk account.

PilotDesk does not claim to reproduce the FAA’s confidential active item bank. The purpose is to cover the current standards deeply enough that the user has to understand the underlying regulation, calculation, system, weather concept or decision instead of recognizing an answer from memory.
