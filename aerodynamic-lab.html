What the numbers mean

Dynamic pressure q = ½ρV². Lift L = qSCL. Drag D = qS(CD₀ + CL² / (πeAR)), with AR = b²/S. Reynolds number = ρVc/μ. Mach = V/√(γRT), assuming dry air with γ = 1.4 and R = 287.05 J/(kg·K).

Doubling speed multiplies lift and drag by four when density and coefficients stay fixed. A longer span at the same area reduces induced drag for the same CL and efficiency. CL and CD₀ depend on the aircraft, angle of attack and flow conditions; this model does not predict stall, separation or a complete aircraft's performance.

Where OpenFOAM fits

OpenFOAM solves a flow field around a specified geometry. The Inductiva wind-tunnel repository provides an off-browser workflow for preparing and running those cases. Its example results are low resolution. No CFD run is connected to this page yet.

A usable CFD result needs documented geometry, reference area, boundary conditions, mesh convergence, solver convergence and comparison with relevant wind-tunnel data. A solver result alone does not establish accuracy.
