A practical workflow

Choose the airplane first. Build and review the loading. Plot the route and navlog. Check endpoint weather and current official sources. Attach the terminal procedures you expect to brief. Use imported POH performance models only after verifying their trace against the applicable source. Training material stays in the aircraft file rather than becoming a generic checklist pile.

Source visibility is intentional

FAA raster chart imagery remains FAA-hosted. FAA terminal procedures remain the FAA d-TPP PDFs. Current AWC weather is requested from PilotDesk's live server endpoint and is not intentionally served from the offline cache. Aircraft-specific performance and loading data come from the user and should be checked against the current approved information applicable to that aircraft.
