PilotDesk

PilotDesk is a fast aviation utility built around the calculations pilots actually reach for: crosswind, fuel, altitude, descent, turns, navigation, weight and balance, conversions and more.

Why it exists

A lot of pilot math is simple but repetitive. PilotDesk puts those calculations in one mobile-friendly place so a pilot or student can get the arithmetic quickly, see the method, and move on. No account is required for the core calculators.

More than calculators

PilotDesk also includes live METAR and TAF lookup, runway wind-component assistance when airport data is available, locally stored aircraft profiles, favorites, recent calculations and a reusable Weight & Balance Builder.

Built around verification

PilotDesk is intentionally not presented as an authority. Calculator outputs are planning aids. Weather is supplemental. Aircraft-specific limitations and performance come from current approved data. When PilotDesk and an approved source disagree, the approved source controls.

Privacy and monetization

The site is supported by advertising. Aircraft profiles and calculator convenience data are stored locally on the user's device rather than in a PilotDesk account database. Aggregate site analytics help identify what tools people use and which pages need improvement.

Keep it useful

If something looks wrong, report it. If there is a calculator pilots would actually use, suggest it. The goal is not to become an overloaded flight-planning suite; it is to stay quick, clear and useful.
