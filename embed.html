Linking and attribution

Policy-safe setup

The widget does not require a ranking-credit backlink. If your school, club, or aviation site genuinely recommends PilotDesk, you are welcome to link editorially to the calculator, flight-training tools, aviation guides, or the online E6B from your own resource page. There is no reciprocal-link requirement.

This keeps the embedded tool useful for visitors while leaving any normal website recommendation up to the publisher.

How it works

Small and simple
Client-side math

The calculation runs in the visitor's browser. The widget does not need an account.

Shareable handoff

Open in PilotDesk carries the entered values into the full calculator and tags the referral source.

Safe site boundary

The widget is a script, not an iframe, and Shadow DOM isolates its styling.
