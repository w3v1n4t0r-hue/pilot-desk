How to use the 3° Descent Rate calculator

Enter groundspeed to calculate the vertical speed for a three-degree path and compare it with the common groundspeed-times-five estimate.

Inputs

  • Groundspeed — enter the value in kt.

Formula and method

Vertical speed = groundspeed × 6,076.1 ÷ 60 × tan(3°). The familiar groundspeed × 5 rule is a cockpit approximation.

What the result means

The exact geometric rate and cockpit shortcut should be close at ordinary approach speeds, but are not identical.

Common mistakes to avoid

Use groundspeed because the airplane covers ground distance at that rate. Follow published vertical guidance and procedure constraints.
