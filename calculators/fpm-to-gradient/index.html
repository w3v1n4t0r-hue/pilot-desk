How to use the FPM to Climb Gradient calculator

FPM to Climb Gradient: Use this to understand the relationship or check arithmetic. The aircraft’s current POH or AFM has the performance numbers for a real flight.

Inputs

  • Vertical speed — enter the value in fpm.
  • Groundspeed — enter the value in kt.

Formula and method

Climb gradient (ft/NM) = vertical speed (ft/min) ÷ groundspeed (NM/min).

What the result means

Gradient: A math result helps compare conditions, but cannot account for every aircraft configuration, runway surface or technique.

Common mistakes to avoid

FPM to Climb Gradient check: Check the weight, configuration, surface and atmospheric assumptions against the POH or AFM chart; a shortcut may not cover them all.
