3° Descent Rate Chart

If you know groundspeed, you can get a useful target vertical speed in a few seconds. The table shows the exact three-degree geometry beside the common groundspeed-times-five shortcut so you can see the difference.

Exact three-degree geometry versus the quick rule

For a three-degree flight path, vertical speed depends on groundspeed. The geometric calculation is groundspeed in NM per minute × 6,076.1 ft/NM × tan(3°). In normal light-airplane ranges, that works out near 5.3 × groundspeed. The familiar groundspeed × 5 rule is easier to do in your head and intentionally runs a little lower.
