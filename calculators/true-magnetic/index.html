How to use the True / Magnetic Heading calculator

Enter a direction and its reference, then apply the local magnetic variation with a consistent east/west sign.

Inputs

  • Direction value — enter the value in °.
  • Variation (east + / west −) — enter the value in °.

Formula and method

True and magnetic direction differ by magnetic variation; the sign convention must stay consistent through the conversion.

What the result means

The two outputs show the equivalent true and magnetic direction under the selected variation convention.

Common mistakes to avoid

Confirm whether the source is true or magnetic before applying variation. A sign reversal can shift the answer by twice the variation.
