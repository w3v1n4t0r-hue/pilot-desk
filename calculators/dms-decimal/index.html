How to use the DMS / Decimal Degrees calculator

Enter degrees, minutes, seconds, and hemisphere, or provide decimal degrees for the reverse conversion.

Inputs

  • Degrees — enter the value in deg.
  • Minutes — enter the value in min.
  • Seconds — enter the value in sec.
  • Sign (1 north/east, -1 south/west).

Formula and method

Decimal degrees = degrees + minutes ÷ 60 + seconds ÷ 3,600, with the appropriate north/south or east/west sign.

What the result means

The signed decimal result carries the hemisphere: north/east are positive and south/west are negative. The DMS output is normalized into minutes and seconds.

Common mistakes to avoid

Minutes and seconds must be below 60. Confirm latitude and longitude were not swapped, and preserve the west/south sign when moving coordinates between systems.
