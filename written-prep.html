Free FAA written test prep with 5,000 ACS-linked practice questions

PilotDesk Written Prep contains 1,000 practice questions each for Private Pilot Airplane (PAR), Instrument Rating Airplane (IRA), Commercial Pilot Airplane (CAX), Flight Instructor Airplane (FIA), and Airline Transport Pilot Multiengine Airplane (ATM). The questions are tied to the applicable current FAA ACS document and knowledge element. An additional 250 CFII questions use FAA-S-8081-9E because the FAA currently keeps Flight Instructor Instrument under the PTS rather than an ACS.

Every question uses exactly three answer choices. The bank varies numbers and scenarios so pilots have to work the rule, calculation, system, weather concept or decision instead of memorizing one answer. Foundation, applied and advanced filters let you raise the difficulty. Missed-question review, scores by FAA standard and practice exams are saved to a free PilotDesk account.

PilotDesk does not claim to reproduce the FAA’s confidential active question bank. Use current FAA regulations, ACS or PTS documents, handbooks and testing supplements as the controlling sources.
