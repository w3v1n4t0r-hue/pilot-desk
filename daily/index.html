Daily pilot quiz without a long study session

PilotDesk Daily is a short daily aviation quiz for repetition. Challenges rotate through METAR decoding, TAF concepts, density altitude, crosswind reasoning, weight and balance, pitot-static systems, airport surface awareness, icing awareness and aeronautical decision making.

Want to study the same subjects in more depth? Use the METAR and TAF guide, aviation weather reference, pilot math formula reference, or the multi-engine checkride study guide. Those pages connect the quick daily questions to longer explanations and working PilotDesk tools.

You can play as a guest. A PilotDesk account can save Daily completions and study progress, but the daily check itself stays free.
