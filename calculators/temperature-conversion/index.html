How to use the Temperature Conversion calculator

Enter a temperature in Celsius, Fahrenheit, or Kelvin to convert it to the other scales.

Inputs

  • Celsius — enter the value in °C.

Formula and method

Celsius and Fahrenheit use different zero points and scale sizes; Kelvin is an absolute temperature scale.

What the result means

Celsius and Fahrenheit have different zero points; Kelvin starts at absolute zero. The outputs are equivalent temperatures.

Common mistakes to avoid

Confirm the source scale before entering a value. A Celsius/Fahrenheit mix-up can be large enough to invalidate weather or performance work.
