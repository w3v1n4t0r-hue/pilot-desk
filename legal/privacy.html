PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026. PilotDesk collects as little personal information as practical for the features it provides. Most personal app data stays in your browser rather than being stored in a PilotDesk account.

Information stored on your device

Aircraft profiles, favorites, recently used tools, recent calculation summaries, saved scenarios, usage preferences and similar convenience settings are stored in your browser's local storage. PilotDesk does not send aircraft-profile values or calculator inputs to a PilotDesk account or PilotDesk application database during normal use. Clearing PilotDesk site data in your browser may remove those locally stored items.

If you use an export feature, your browser creates the exported file on your device. PilotDesk does not receive that file unless you independently choose to send or upload it somewhere else.

Weather and airport requests

When you request airport weather or airport-search information, the station identifier or search request is sent to a PilotDesk server endpoint so the requested aviation data can be retrieved. Normal request metadata may appear in hosting or security logs. Do not enter personal information into airport or weather search fields.

Analytics and performance

PilotDesk uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to understand site traffic and real-world performance. These services may process information such as page views, referrers, browser/device type, general location derived by the service, and page-performance measurements. PilotDesk also records limited product events such as a calculator being used, a weather search, a Quick Start choice, a favorite being changed, an aircraft profile action, an install prompt being used, or a guide leading to a calculator.

These product events identify the feature or page, not the numerical values you entered. PilotDesk is configured not to send aircraft names, tail numbers, passenger information, calculator inputs, Weight & Balance entries, search text, or raw aircraft-profile data as analytics-event properties.

Advertising and consent

PilotDesk uses Google AdSense to help support the free site. Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to serve, measure and, where permitted, personalize advertising. Where required, a consent message may provide choices about advertising and related data processing. Google's policies and consent controls apply to Google's services.

PilotDesk does not intentionally provide Google Ads with aircraft-profile values, calculator inputs, Weight & Balance entries, passenger information, or tail numbers for ad targeting.

Error reporting

To find broken pages and JavaScript failures, PilotDesk may send a limited technical error report to hosting logs. The report format is intended to include technical error information and the page path while excluding aircraft profiles, calculator inputs and form contents.

Server and security logs

Hosting, security and advertising providers may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, requested URL, referrer and security-related information as part of operating their services. Retention and handling of provider-level logs are controlled by the applicable provider policies and settings.

Accounts, payments and data sales

PilotDesk currently does not require a PilotDesk account and does not directly collect payment-card information. PilotDesk does not sell aircraft-profile data or calculator-input data. If account, cloud-sync, or paid features are added later, this policy will be updated before those features are relied on for personal data or billing.

Your controls

You can remove locally stored PilotDesk data by clearing the site's storage in your browser. Browser privacy controls and applicable Google consent controls can also limit cookies or advertising-related processing. Blocking advertising is not intended to disable PilotDesk's core calculator or saved-data features.

Public feedback reports

The PilotDesk feedback page can open a GitHub issue for you to review before posting. GitHub issues may be public. Do not include passwords, private passenger information, sensitive personal information, or security exploit details in a public report.

External links

PilotDesk links to third-party sources, including FAA and other government aviation resources. Once you leave PilotDesk, the destination site's policies apply.

Children

PilotDesk is a general aviation utility and is not directed to children under 13. PilotDesk does not knowingly create child accounts or knowingly collect personal information from children through an account system.

Changes

This policy may be updated as PilotDesk changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Questions

Privacy questions can be submitted through the PilotDesk feedback page. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in a public report.

This page describes PilotDesk's current practices. Third-party providers control their own systems, retention periods and policies.
