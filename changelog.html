What’s New

September 2026

PilotDesk added shareable calculator URLs, Copy Result and Copy Link tools, better mobile result handling, saved calculator inputs, aircraft profiles, improved local history, and stronger error handling.

Core calculator pages now include clearer formulas, assumptions, examples, source guidance, and additional aviation-safety context. Major tools also include short FAQ sections covering standard aviation relationships and reminders to verify aircraft-specific values with the current POH or AFM.

Offline support was expanded so core math tools remain available when connectivity drops. Live METAR and TAF data remain network-dependent and are explicitly marked unavailable when offline rather than being presented as current weather.

Accessibility and cockpit usability also improved with System, Light, Dark, and Night Red themes, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, clearer focus states, and print-friendly calculator output.

PilotDesk is an educational planning aid and is not FAA approved. Operational decisions must use current approved aircraft data, charts, weather, NOTAMs, procedures, and regulations as applicable.
