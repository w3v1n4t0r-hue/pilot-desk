For instructors

Use a PilotDesk page as the starting point for a question, not the answer sheet. Give the student the route, weather, loading, or performance problem first. Have them make an estimate and explain the decision, then use the calculator or guide to check the work. If the number is aircraft-specific, go back to the actual POH or AFM together.

What PilotDesk is not

PilotDesk does not replace the ACS, current regulations, the AIM, official weather, charts, NOTAMs, or the aircraft POH/AFM. Start with the FAA ACS page and current FAA aviation handbooks.

Check the current FAA publication before a knowledge test, practical test, or flight. FAA standards and publications can change. Verify the current version before a knowledge test, practical test, or flight.
