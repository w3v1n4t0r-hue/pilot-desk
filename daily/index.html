HOW IT WORKS

Same challenge for everyone.

TRAINING NOTE

Practice, not operational authority.

PilotDesk Daily is educational practice. Verify current regulations, procedures, weather and aircraft information with official or approved sources before flight.

Daily aviation practice without a long study session

PilotDesk Daily is a short daily pilot quiz for repetition. Challenges rotate through METAR decoding, TAF concepts, density altitude, crosswind reasoning, weight and balance, pitot-static systems, airport surface awareness, icing awareness and aeronautical decision making.

You can play as a guest. A PilotDesk account adds saved scores, XP, levels, badges and daily streaks, but the challenge itself stays free.
