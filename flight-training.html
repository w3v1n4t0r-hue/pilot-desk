Use one scenario across the whole lesson

Take a real airplane and a real route. Check the weather. Build the loading. Work the wind, time, fuel, and descent numbers. Brief the airport and procedures. Then change one thing: a stronger wind, a passenger, higher temperature, lower ceiling, failed alternator, or engine failure. The follow-up decision is usually where the useful studying happens.

What PilotDesk is not

It is not a replacement for the ACS, current regulations, the AIM, official weather, charts, NOTAMs, or the aircraft POH/AFM. The FAA says practical tests use the ACS in effect on the day of the test. Start with the FAA ACS page and the current FAA aviation handbooks, then use PilotDesk as the scratch paper and study workspace around those sources.

Source review: September 2026. FAA standards and publications can change. Verify the current version before a practical test or flight.
