DMS and decimal-degree coordinates

Aviation coordinates may be written as degrees, minutes, and seconds or as decimal degrees. Both formats describe the same latitude or longitude, but software, charts, GPS systems, and databases may use different formats.

DMS to decimal degrees

Use decimal degrees = degrees + minutes ÷ 60 + seconds ÷ 3600. For example, 47° 30' 00" becomes 47.5000°.

Hemisphere signs

North and east coordinates are normally positive. South and west coordinates are negative in signed decimal-degree format. A longitude of 97° west becomes approximately -97.0000°.

Decimal degrees to DMS

The whole-number portion gives degrees. Multiply the remaining decimal by 60 to get minutes, then multiply the remaining minute fraction by 60 to get seconds.

Why precision matters

Small coordinate errors can move a point significantly on the ground, especially when seconds or decimal places are entered incorrectly. Always verify that the format expected by your chart, database, or navigation system matches the format you are entering.
