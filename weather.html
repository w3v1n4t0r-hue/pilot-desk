Weather data behavior

PilotDesk requests data server-side from the Aviation Weather Center rather than making browser cross-origin requests. The endpoint retries temporary AWC failures, can continue with partial METAR/TAF/station results, and does not intentionally serve live weather from the PilotDesk offline service-worker cache.

If AWC is temporarily unavailable or rate-limited, PilotDesk shows that failure instead of presenting old weather as current.
