FLIGHT SCHOOLS

Free Aviation Tools for Flight Schools & CFIs

PilotDesk gives instructors and student pilots a browser-based collection of aviation calculators and study references that can be opened without installing an app.

Tools for ground lessons

Flight schools can use dedicated pages for common aviation-math subjects such as crosswind components, density altitude, weight and balance concepts, climb performance, navigation math and E6B-style calculations.

Student pilot study resources

The PilotDesk Flight Training hub organizes training-oriented references together so students can move from a concept explanation to a related calculator without hunting through unrelated pages.

Website embeds for flight schools

Schools, instructors and flying clubs that maintain their own websites can also use PilotDesk's aviation calculator widgets. The embed page is designed to make useful pilot tools easier to distribute where students already study.

Popular starting points

Useful links include the crosswind calculator, density altitude calculator, online E6B flight computer, checklist trainer, and aviation guide library.

Why PilotDesk is useful for instructors

Each tool has its own shareable URL. That makes it easy to send a student directly to the topic being discussed rather than asking them to navigate a large multipurpose app.
