3 degree descent rate formula

Vertical speed = GS × 6,076.12 ÷ 60 × tan(3°)

That is approximately 5.3 × groundspeed. The common GS × 5 rule is easier to use mentally and provides a quick starting estimate.

3 to 1 descent rule

For descent distance, a common planning shortcut is about 3 NM per 1,000 feet to lose. The vertical-speed calculation and 3:1 distance rule are complementary planning tools, but neither overrides a charted procedure or altitude restriction.

3 degree descent questions

What rate is a 3° descent at 120 knots?

About 637 fpm using exact geometry. The GS × 5 shortcut gives 600 fpm.

What rate is it at 100 knots?

About 531 fpm exact, or 500 fpm with the quick rule.

Why use groundspeed?

The required descent rate must match the airplane's movement over the ground. Wind changes groundspeed and therefore the FPM needed to hold the same path angle.
