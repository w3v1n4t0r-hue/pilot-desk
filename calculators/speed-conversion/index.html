How to use the Speed Conversion calculator

Enter a speed and choose its source unit. The page returns equivalents commonly used in aviation and general measurement.

Inputs

  • Knots — enter the value in kt.

Formula and method

One knot equals one nautical mile per hour, approximately 1.15078 mph, 1.852 km/h or 0.51444 m/s.

What the result means

The outputs are unit conversions, not wind corrections or airspeed corrections. The underlying speed remains unchanged.

Common mistakes to avoid

Check whether the original value is knots, mph, km/h, or meters per second before copying it into another tool.
