Free FAA written test prep without a paid question-bank subscription

PilotDesk Written Prep is designed for pilots studying for the Private Pilot Airplane (PAR), Instrument Rating Airplane (IRA), Commercial Pilot Airplane (CAX), Flight Instructor Airplane (FIA), Flight Instructor Instrument Airplane (FII), and Airline Transport Pilot Multiengine Airplane (ATM) knowledge tests. The FAA’s active item bank is not public, so PilotDesk does not claim to reproduce confidential live exam questions. Instead, the study bank is built from FAA-published sample-question concepts, current regulations, FAA handbooks, ACS/PTS knowledge areas, and original FAA-style practice items.

The FAA currently lists 60 questions for PAR, 60 for IRA, 100 for CAX, 100 for FIA, 50 for FII, and 125 for ATM, with a 70 percent passing score. PilotDesk practice-exam sessions are shorter study simulations and clearly show the real FAA test size beside them.
