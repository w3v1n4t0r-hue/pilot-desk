Basic flight fuel math

Fuel planning starts with simple arithmetic, but safe and legal fuel planning requires much more than multiplying cruise burn by flight time.

Trip fuel

Trip fuel = Estimated fuel burn × Estimated flight time

Use a realistic burn rate for the phase of flight and aircraft configuration. Taxi, climb, cruise, descent, approach and expected delays may use different amounts of fuel.

Reserve fuel

Reserve fuel = Fuel burn × Reserve time

The reserve entered in a calculator is only an arithmetic input. Applicable regulations, operating rules and company procedures determine the actual reserve requirement.

Fuel remaining

Fuel margin = Fuel on board − Trip fuel − Planned reserve

A positive margin does not automatically make a flight legal or prudent. Weather, alternates, reroutes, ATC delays, unusable fuel, fuel-system limitations and aircraft-specific procedures all matter.

Endurance versus range

Endurance is time; range is distance. Wind changes range because it changes groundspeed, even if fuel flow stays the same.
