What this brief is — and is not

PilotDesk combines your locally saved planning assumptions with current endpoint weather and quick links to authoritative sources. It is intentionally a convenience workspace, not a substitute for an official weather briefing, flight plan, dispatch release, approved performance source, or regulatory requirement.
