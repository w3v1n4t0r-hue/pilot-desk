PILOTDESK LAB

Flight Planning Lab

Tools built around transparent math and user-verifiable source data. These features are designed to make planning and training easier without pretending to replace approved aircraft data, charts, procedures, or an official briefing.

Route + navlog

Route Planner

Resolve waypoints, plot the route, calculate TC/WCA/TH/MH/GS/ETE/fuel, and show endpoint METAR context.

Aircraft performance

POH Chart Studio

Digitize a chart you legally possess into a deterministic interpolation model with visible calibration and source tracking.

Student training

Checklist Trainer

Import checklist text, hear it read aloud, and practice challenge-response or flow recall without reordering items.

Coming after validation

FAA VFR and IFR raster chart layers, chart-cycle switching, procedure/plate indexing, selected approach plates in a route pack, winds-aloft integration, and richer route weather overlays are all possible. They should be released only after the chart-ingestion and cycle-validity pipeline has been tested against official FAA products.
