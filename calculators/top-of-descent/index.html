How to use the Top of Descent calculator

Top of Descent: Try the values from a route or training scenario. Check that wind direction, course reference, speed, time and fuel units match your source.

Inputs

  • Altitude to lose — enter the value in ft.
  • Descent gradient — enter the value in ft/NM.
  • Groundspeed — enter the value in kt.

Formula and method

Top-of-descent distance = altitude to lose ÷ selected descent gradient. Descent time then follows from groundspeed.

What the result means

TOD distance: Groundspeed affects estimated time and fuel. Wind direction also changes the headwind, tailwind or crosswind component.

Common mistakes to avoid

Top of Descent check: Check whether a calculation calls for airspeed or groundspeed, and convert minutes to hours before multiplying by knots.
